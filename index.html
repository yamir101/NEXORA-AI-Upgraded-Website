GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered

A few details that help you decide what’s right for your business.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. They are starting estimates. The final price depends on the number of pages, features, integrations and the scope we agree on. You’ll get a clear quote before work begins.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the agreed scope, how quickly content and feedback are available, and whether third-party integrations are involved. We’ll discuss an estimated timeline in your proposal.

Does the chatbot on this website use live AI?

No. This page’s demo uses predefined replies and does not send your chat messages to an AI service. A live AI chatbot would need a separate model/API and secure backend setup.

Are domain, hosting and software subscriptions included?

Not by default. Third-party services and recurring charges are discussed separately and included only when they are clearly listed in the agreed proposal.

How do I get started?

Send an enquiry with a short description of your business and what you need. We’ll review it and discuss whether the project is a good fit.
